PROJECT BACKGROUND & ENGINEERING GOALS

About PARK-ALERT: Engineering Modern Roadway Compliance

Urban curbside management in metropolitan corridors has reached critical friction points. PARK-ALERT introduces an end-to-end edge-assisted computer vision pipeline specifically calibrated for Indian traffic environments, guaranteeing zero-touch statutory enforcement under Section 122 and 177 of the Motor Vehicles Act 1988/2019.

The Problem

Urban congestion, illegal curbside parking, and blocked emergency lanes cause severe traffic delays. Manual traffic police ticketing is slow, labor-intensive, and prone to disputes.

Our Solution

PARK-ALERT introduces an end-to-end edge-assisted computer vision pipeline. Using YOLOv8 and ByteTrack, the system automatically tracks vehicle presence within customized no-parking polygons.

Dual-Tier Escalation

Rather than immediately penalizing drivers, the system grants a grace period, issuing a polite warning email at 20 seconds before escalating to a legal penalty at 30 seconds.

Transparency & Closure

Extracted plates via EasyOCR are linked with contact records to generate tamper-proof digital e-Challans, providing an automated settlement workflow with persistent history.
